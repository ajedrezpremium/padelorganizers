# PadelOrganizers — Vision, Mission & Objectives 2026

## Vision 2026

**To become the reference platform for organizing, managing and growing padel tournaments, connecting organizers, clubs, players, sponsors and brands in a single digital ecosystem.**

PadelOrganizers should evolve from a tournament-management tool into the **Operating System for Padel Events**.

## Mission 2026

**To simplify and professionalize padel tournament organization through technology, automation and artificial intelligence, enabling any organizer to create, manage, promote and monetize events quickly, professionally and profitably.**

**Easier · Faster · More Professional · More Profitable**

# 2026 Strategic Objectives

## 1. Become a Leading Padel Platform

Establish PadelOrganizers as a reference platform in **Spain and Portugal**, while preparing international expansion.

**Target: 500+ registered organizers and clubs.**

## 2. Manage 5,000+ Tournaments

Build an infrastructure capable of managing everything from local tournaments to major professional circuits.

**Target: 5,000+ events managed through the platform.**

## 3. Build a Global Player Community

Create a recurring player ecosystem based on digital profiles and sporting data.

**Target: 100,000+ registered players.**

Player profiles should include:

- Sporting profile
- Tournament history
- Ranking
- Results
- Statistics
- Categories
- Performance evolution

## 4. Make AI a Core Differentiator

Develop an **AI Tournament Assistant** capable of helping organizers with:

- Tournament creation
- Category configuration
- Draw generation
- Registration management
- Player communications
- Customer support
- Social-media content
- Results analysis
- Organizer reports
- Scheduling recommendations
- Tournament optimization

## 5. Build a Multi-Revenue Business Model

**SaaS + Fees + Premium + Sponsorship + Marketplace**

| Product | Business Model |
|---|---|
| Basic | Free |
| Pro Organizer | Subscription |
| Club | Subscription |
| Premium | Advanced subscription |
| Tournament Fee | Percentage of registration |
| Sponsorship | Commission |
| Marketplace | Commission |

## 6. Turn Clubs into Strategic Customers

Develop **Padel Club OS**, a dedicated operating platform for clubs.

Core modules:

- Tournaments
- Leagues
- Rankings
- Members
- Reservations
- Communications
- Payments
- Marketing
- Sponsors
- Statistics

## 7. Create a Connected Tournament Network

Launch the **PadelOrganizers Circuit**, connecting tournaments through a unified platform.

**Tournament → Circuit → Ranking → Player → Community → Sponsor**

This creates a much stronger competitive position than a standalone tournament-management application.

# Strategic Positioning

> “Software for organizing padel tournaments.”

The stronger international positioning is:

> **“The Operating System for Padel Events.”**

Spanish positioning:

> **“El sistema operativo del pádel.”**
